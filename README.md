# Multivariate-Time-Series-Analysis-of-QUAD

In this project, We utilize multi-variate time series modelling (Cointegration) to find out if there is association in the financial market indices of the QUAD.  

We use stock index data of the 4 countries- namely Nifty 50, S&P 500, Nikkei 225 and ASX 200 for 10 years from 2013 to 2023 with a weekly frequency and a log transformation. Confirmation of the variables’ order- I(1) led to performance of the Johansen cointegration test and subsequently a Vector Error Correction Model (VECM). The results show that 2 co-integrating relationships among the models existed. Error Variance Decompositions and Impulse Response Functions were also carried out to determine the effects of movements in indices in response to various shocks and impacts on the model. 

The results show that there do exist long run and short run linkages in the model- which can be explained by diplomatic alliances among the countries - such as QUAD, which require strategic alignment which reflects economically and financially.
